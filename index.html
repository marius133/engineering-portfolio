CONTROL STUDIES

Hybrid mobility and flight-control studies

Simulation studies exploring how rotor thrust can assist ground mobility and how closed-loop control can regulate vertical motion.

Ground mobility · Turning

Thrust-assisted turning

Upward rotor thrust partially unloads the wheels and reduces lateral scrub forces. In simulation, rotor assistance reduced turning radius by 78.2% compared with unassisted driving in the evaluated case.

Ground mobility · Incline

Rotor-assisted incline control

Distance measurements estimate the robot's angle relative to the slope, enabling rotor assistance when wheel traction alone becomes insufficient.

Flight control · Vertical

Height-assist control

Added closed-loop vertical control around a height reference to reduce the need for continuous manual vertical-thrust adjustment.

Personal project · MATLAB / Simulink

PPO-based individual rotor control

Developed a PPO policy with four continuous outputs so the network could command each rotor independently rather than relying on a conventional rotor mixer. The figure shows how the learned policy varies each rotor's thrust while tracking the commanded trajectory.

  • Four continuous actions, one per rotor
  • MATLAB / Simulink and PyTorch
  • Reward shaping for tracking, energy use and episode time

ABOUT
