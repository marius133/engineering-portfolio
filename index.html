ABOUT

Engineering across hardware and software

I am a Mechatronics MSc student at the University of Agder with a particular interest in robotics and control systems. My work spans physical prototyping, embedded systems, dynamic modelling, classical control and reinforcement learning.

I enjoy projects where mechanical, electrical and software design have to work together as one system, and where simulation can be connected back to real hardware.
